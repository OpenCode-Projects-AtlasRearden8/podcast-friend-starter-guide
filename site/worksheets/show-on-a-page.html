Worksheet: your show on a page

One page. Fill it in pen-on-paper or plain text — no publishing tools, no gear pages open. This sheet is your show's constitution: when any decision gets hard later (buy something? add a co-host? change topic?), come back to this page first.

How to use: fill slots 1–7 before you record anything. Slot 8 gets updated after your three practice episodes. Use the copy in your own notes app — treat this page as the template, your copy as the living document.

1. The one-sentence promise

Fill in: "[Who it's for] will get [what they'll have/know] from [how often / how long] episodes about [topic]."

Example shape: "Self-published authors will get one AI tool they can actually finish using, every other week, in 20-minute episodes."

5. The LLC lines (if the show lives under your one-person LLC)

  • The show belongs to: [ ] me [ ] the LLC — decided on: ______________
  • What the show is for, in one phrase: _______________________________
  • What the show will never do (write one line of guardrails): _______________________________
  • Where records and files live (folder path / backup locations): _______________________________
  • Contact email published in the feed: _______________________________

6. The spend rule

  • Money allowed before three practice episodes exist: $0
  • First thing I'm allowed to buy after episode 3 (Chapter 2): _______________________________
  • Hardware already in this project: _______________________________

7. Launch number

Episodes that will exist before I tell anyone: [ ] 3 (recommended) [ ] other: ______ Launch month (honest): _______________________________

8. Revised after three practice episodes

What broke (voice, room, promises I couldn't keep)? _______________________________ The one purchase that actually earned it: _______________________________ Promise sentence, final version: _______________________________

Keep this page with your notes. When anything about the show feels confusing, reread slot 1 — the promise you made on paper is the compass, not the stats app.
