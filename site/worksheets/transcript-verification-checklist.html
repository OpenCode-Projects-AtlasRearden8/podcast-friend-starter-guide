Worksheet: transcript and show-notes verification checklist

Run this checklist every time before AI-drafted or AI-assisted transcripts, show notes, timestamps, or episode titles publish. It takes about ten minutes per episode and comes from Chapter 5 (guest episodes add the loop from Chapter 6).

The rule at the top of the whole chapter: AI drafts; humans verify; nothing publishes unreviewed.

Source material in front of you

  • ☐ Final edited audio open and playable
  • ☐ Draft transcript open
  • ☐ Draft show notes open
  • ☐ Your episode planner (for the promise and intended claims)
  • ☐ Guest, if any: their spoken-claims list from the recording notes

A. Names (the most common AI error)

  • ☐ Every proper noun in notes and transcript checked against the audio
    • Guest's name, spelled exactly the way they spell it (they told you in the prep email, or you asked on the call)
    • Tool, company, and app names (a wrong name in notes is invisible until a listener Googles it)
  • ☐ Book titles, course names, communities mentioned — verified as written
  • ☐ Acronyms expanded correctly (KDP, RSS, LUFS...) at first use in the notes

B. Numbers and claims

  • ☐ Every number in the notes (prices, counts, minutes, percentages) matches the audio
  • ☐ Every factual claim ("studies show," "according to") — opened the actual source, or rewritten the line to say less
  • ☐ No invented links: anything cited exists at the URL given, and you visited it today (Chapter 5's links-last rule)
  • ☐ Your own episode's promise (from the planner) is what the notes say the episode delivers

C. Quotes

  • ☐ Every quoted sentence (from you or a guest) was played back from the actual audio, not taken from the AI draft
  • ☐ Guest quotes feel safe to publish out of context — if in doubt, ask them while sending the publish plan (Chapter 6)
  • ☐ Nothing in the notes is a smoother, shorter version of a sentence nobody actually said

D. Timestamps

  • ☐ Spot-check three: start, one middle, end
  • ☐ If two or more drift more than roughly half a minute, regenerate and re-check

E. Words and voice

  • ☐ Read notes aloud once; rewrite any sentences that don't sound like you
  • ☐ Clear "um" chains and repetitions in the transcript text only where it helps readers — the aired audio is untouched
  • ☐ Guest-sensitive passages (vulnerable or offhand moments) are cut, or confirmed with the guest first

F. Honesty line

  • ☐ One line states, plainly, that AI drafted something and a human checked it, e.g. "Transcript drafted by a tool, checked by hand"
  • ☐ Corrections policy: if a mistake slips out anyway, the correction is published in a visible place — Chapter 6's disputes note is the model

G. Ship

  • ☐ Verified notes pasted into the host's episode form (the host copy is the one people read)
  • ☐ Verified transcript filed with the episode in your folder tree (Chapter 2 syncing habit)
  • ☐ Planner page archived under its episode number — your searchable archive grows one file at a time

Count A–G at the end: all boxes checked. If the same box stays unchecked for two episodes in a row, reduce AI drafting on that step rather than adding editing speed. The checklist is the product; the transcript is just paper around it.
