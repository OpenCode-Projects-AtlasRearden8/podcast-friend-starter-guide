Worksheet: episode planner

Fill this in two or three times per recording morning, before you press record (see Chapter 4). Ten minutes of filling saves an hour of editing. Keep working copies with your recording files; this page is the template you copy each time.

The rule: if you cannot fill item 3 (the cold-open sentence) in one try, the episode idea is not ready — pick another from the ten list on your show-on-a-page sheet.
