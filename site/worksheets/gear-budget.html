Worksheet: gear and budget (buy-nothing-first)

This sheet exists to keep money where it belongs: behind the show, not ahead of it. Rules come from Chapters 1 and 2; the discipline is here, on paper, where impulse shopping has to argue with itself.

Phase 0: already owned (before three practice episodes)

Inventory what you already have. Do not buy anything on this list:

  • ☐ Phone (any model from the last several years) — voice memo app located and tested
  • ☐ Wired earbuds or headphones (any kind)
  • ☐ A soft thing: blanket, closet with clothes, sofa cushions, curtains
  • ☐ A quiet 25-minute window you found by listening to your rooms
  • ☐ Water glass that doesn't click

Planned practice episodes with this setup:

  • Episode 1 (date): ____________ What broke: _______________________________
  • Episode 2 (date): ____________ What broke: _______________________________
  • Episode 3 (date): ____________ What broke: _______________________________

Phase 1: the one earned purchase (after episode 3)

Item Budget Buy only if... Your note
Dynamic USB microphone $40–$70 ...voice quality is the big remaining complaint after 3 episodes
Small desk stand or boom arm $0–$25 (often included) ...the mic keeps drifting on your desk
Basic wired headphones (if you truly own none) ~$20 ...you listen back on speakers right now

Phase 1 total: about $60–$115, and only on evidence from three recordings.

Not on the phase-1 list (revisit at episode 10+, one qualified need at a time): audio interface, XLR cables, mixer, condenser microphone, acoustic foam panels, second mic "for guests," anything with a superhero name.

Phase 2: earned by the show, not by you

(The show earns it via published episodes and range; not credit-card enthusiasm.)

  • ☐ Dedicated hosting on a paid plan (Chapter 3) — when its free tier genuinely annoys you
  • ☐ Mobile apps, each with a rule: record something before you keep it, delete it after a week if idle
  • ☐ Design help on cover art — when the free one is the weakest thing in your listing

Keep this sheet next to your show-on-a-page worksheet. Two sheets, one folder, zero impulse purchases.

Free software inventory (all zero dollars)

  • ☐ Recording: phone voice memo app / Voice Memos / a free macOS or Linux equivalent
  • ☐ Editing: Audacity (or GarageBand on Mac) — installed, and the four skills from Chapter 3 practiced once
  • ☐ Hosting: a free tier of one host (Chapter 3), chosen after reading two current pricing pages
  • ☐ Transcription: local Whisper (Chapter 5), if it fits your machine; nothing with a monthly subscription until you transcribe more than the free tier allows
  • ☐ Worksheet files: these four pages, copied into your notes folder

The one-page promise

I will not spend more than $________ on this podcast until ______ episodes exist that I am proud of. (Suggestion: $0 until three episodes exist; then, and only then, phase 1's one microphone.)

Sign it, date it, put it where the shopping tab opens from. When the urge to upgrade arrives, write down what specific three-episode problem motivates it. If no specific problem surfaces, the urge is enthusiasm wearing a receipt — close the tab, record episode 4 instead.
