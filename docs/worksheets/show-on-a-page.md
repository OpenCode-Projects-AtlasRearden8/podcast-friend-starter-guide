# Worksheet: your show on a page

One page. Fill it in pen-on-paper or plain text — no publishing tools, no gear pages open. This sheet is your show's constitution: when any decision gets hard later (buy something? add a co-host? change topic?), come back to this page first.

> How to use: fill slots 1–7 before you record anything. Slot 8 gets updated after your three practice episodes. Use the copy in your own notes app — treat this page as the template, your copy as the living document.
