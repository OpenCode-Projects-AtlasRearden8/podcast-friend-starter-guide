# Worksheet: episode planner

Fill this in two or three times per recording morning, *before* you press record (see [Chapter 4](../04-episode-structure-you-can-sustain.md)). Ten minutes of filling saves an hour of editing. Keep working copies with your recording files; this page is the template you copy each time.

> The rule: if you cannot fill item 3 (the cold-open sentence) in one try, the episode idea is not ready — pick another from the ten list on your show-on-a-page sheet.

Keep filled planners together in your notes folder, named ep##-planner. Six months from now, this stack is also the outline of what you learned — a record of your thinking, and the easiest source for a "best of" finale if the day comes ([Chapter 8](../08-quitting-politely.md)).
